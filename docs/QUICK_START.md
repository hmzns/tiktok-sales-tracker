# TikTok Sales Tracker — Quick Start

Use this checklist when managing daily sales, stock, expenses, and TikTok orders.

## 1. Start of Day

Open the tracker and check the Dashboard.

Check:

- Orders
- Revenue
- Net Profit
- Orders Needing Items
- Stock Activity

## 2. Check Products and Stock

Go to **Products**.

Before selling:

- Make sure the products you sell exist.
- Check stock quantities.
- Add new products when necessary.
- Update stock when necessary.

**Important:** Do not manually deduct stock after completing an order. The tracker deducts stock automatically when the order is completed.

## 3. Sync TikTok Orders

Go to **Orders** and press:

**Sync TikTok Orders**

The tracker checks TikTok for new eligible orders.

Orders before **September 1, 2026** are intentionally not imported.

New TikTok orders normally appear as **Needs Items**.

If no new orders appear, that does not necessarily mean there is an error.

## 4. Complete a Needs Items Order

Open the **Needs Items** order.

1. Search for the product.
2. Select the actual product sold.
3. Enter the quantity.
4. Check the available stock.
5. Select the payment method.
6. Review the order.
7. Complete the order.

### Payment Methods

**Full TikTok Payment**

The buyer paid for the product and shipping through TikTok Shop.

**Product Paid Outside TikTok**

The buyer paid for the product outside TikTok, such as WhatsApp, while TikTok handled the TikTok-side order/shipping payment.

## 5. Full TikTok Payment

For a **Full TikTok Payment** order:

- Product selection identifies what was sold and deducts stock.
- The normal product price in the tracker is not the final TikTok financial value.
- TikTok Finance provides the final TikTok settlement.
- Profit may initially show as **Pending**.
- Once the order is settled by TikTok, the tracker can calculate the final profit.

Do not manually calculate TikTok taxes or fees.

## 6. Product Paid Outside TikTok

For a **Product Paid Outside TikTok** order:

- The tracker uses the product-side payment information recorded in the tracker.
- Discounts can be applied when appropriate.
- TikTok Finance provides the TikTok-side settlement information.

## 7. Discounts

For orders using the tracker product-payment flow, apply the discount before completing the order.

Available options:

- No Discount
- Fixed Amount
- Percentage

Always check the final amount before confirming.

## 8. Order Status

**Needs Items**  
Product details still need to be added.

**Completed**  
The order has been completed and stock has been processed.

**Refunded**  
The order was refunded.

**Cancelled**  
The order was cancelled.

## 9. Expenses

Go to **Expenses**.

To record spending:

1. Press **Add Expense**.
2. Enter the amount.
3. Enter the description.
4. Select the appropriate category.
5. Select the correct expense date.
6. Save.

Use **Edit** or **Delete** when necessary.

## 10. Reports

Go to **Reports** when you want to review business performance.

**Overview**  
General business summary.

**Product Performance**  
Shows product sales and financial performance when the required financial data is available.

**Sales Trends**  
Shows sales performance over time.

## 11. TikTok Sync Status

Open **TikTok Sync Status** to check:

- Last successful sync
- Latest sync result
- Sync history
- Failed attempts

**Sync TikTok Orders** remains the normal way to manually retrieve new orders.

## 12. Important Rules

- Do not manually deduct stock after completing an order.
- Do not complete the same order twice.
- Do not manually change TikTok settlement values.
- Do not treat buyer shipping fee as seller profit.
- Orders before September 1, 2026 are intentionally not imported.
- If TikTok Finance shows **Pending**, wait for settlement instead of entering a value manually.

## 13. If Something Looks Wrong

**An order is missing**  
Try **Sync TikTok Orders** and check the TikTok Sync Status.

**A product is faded**  
The product may be inactive or out of stock.

**Profit is pending**  
TikTok Finance may not have settled the order yet.

**Stock looks wrong**  
Do not immediately change the stock. Check the order and Stock Activity first.

For persistent problems, contact the system administrator.

- [ ] Completed orders checked
- [ ] Expenses recorded
- [ ] Stock looks correct
- [ ] Dashboard checked
